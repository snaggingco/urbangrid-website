---
name: Public report privacy
description: Privacy checks and interpretation of historical inspection samples
---
Public availability is not evidence that an inspection report is safe to promote. Check the underlying text, metadata and visible images for client names and precise property identifiers. Redaction must permanently remove underlying content, not just cover it visually.

**Why:** An existing publicly accessible sample contained identifying details even though it was presented as a marketing download.

**How to apply:** Before adding sample-download pathways or replacing reports, review both extraction and rendered pages. Keep private originals and redaction term files outside the public repository. Historical report terms are illustrative, not authoritative for current payment or inspection scope. A workspace-only correction does not remove the older published file; do not publish without the user's permission.
