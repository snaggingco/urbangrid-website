Treat assistant knowledge as part of the public claims review. Do not let a historical sales persona reintroduce approvals, qualifications, guarantees or invented sample findings removed from the website.

**Why:** An audit found that the legacy assistant still asserted unverified credentials, unconditional DLP repair obligations and obsolete report timing, and fabricated sample findings rather than referring to the actual report.

**How to apply:** Ground replies in current approved facts and use controlled responses for sensitive credential, payment/report, DLP and sample-report questions. Reuse authoritative pricing; never let the model perform billing arithmetic. A marketing statement is not issuer-verifiable credential evidence.

## Verified company registration

The owner subsequently supplied documentary evidence of RERA real estate office registration. Company-level office registration is now substantiated; do not continue a blanket denial of all RERA status.

**Why:** The certificate establishes the registered legal entity and activities, not RERA approval, endorsement, certification, approval of individual reports or individual professional qualifications.

**How to apply:** Use “RERA Registered” or the precise office-registration wording. Keep certificate validity explicit. MRICS/InterNACHI/ISO/Dubai Municipality and individual credentials require separate evidence. Do not recreate a certificate image when only textual certificate facts were supplied.
