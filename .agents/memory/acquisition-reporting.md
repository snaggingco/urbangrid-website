---
name: Acquisition reporting scope
description: Cohort and attribution decisions for internal acquisition-to-revenue reporting; external ownership of Ads spend.
---

Use lead-created cohorts in inclusive Dubai calendar dates for acquisition-to-booking analysis. Use a single attribution model across lead and financial measures; the internal Acquisition report intentionally uses first-touch acquisition, not the bookings screen's last-touch model.

**Why:** Comparing lead-created cohorts with booking-created cohorts produces a misleading conversion rate. First touch answers which acquisition originally generated the enquiry; it is not a claim of agreement with Google Ads' attribution model.

**How to apply:** Label the cohort and model explicitly, retain subsequent booking/payment activity with its originating enquiry, and explain differences when comparing the two existing internal views or external Ads reports.

Google Ads spend and cost per booked inspection are joined externally through ChatGPT/connected Ads reporting. Do not add internal spend entry or app-calculated Ads cost per booking unless the user explicitly expands the scope.

**Why:** The user chose external spend joining and specifically excluded a manual spend feature from the internal business-operations work.

**How to apply:** Provide clearly defined acquisition counts and booking/payment-ledger values for external matching. Do not overwrite staff-recorded lead revenue with payment cash or assume manual lead revenue is equivalent to collected cash.
