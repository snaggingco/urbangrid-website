---
name: Residential booking and payment terms
description: Business rules separating standard residential bookings from custom projects and prohibiting upfront residential payments
---

Standard single-property residential inspections, including normal apartment/villa snagging, DLP/warranty, resale/secondary-market, move-in/move-out and large villas, require no upfront deposit. Confirm the booking without payment. The exact term is: “100% payment after inspection and before release of the final report.”

**Why:** The user corrected an earlier deposit-based design. Do not restore deposit or 50/50 payment terms.

**How to apply:** Staff must mark the physical inspection completed before requesting or recording payment. Paid/payment-cleared status must reflect actual verified or staff-recorded money. Clearance permits report release but must not itself send/publish the report.

Whole-building/common-area work, RFS, BCS/BCA, RCA, commercial surveys, multi-unit projects, consultancy and all other nonstandard services require a Custom Quote lead-capture workflow; UrbanGrid contacts the client.

**Why:** The user explicitly separated these assignments from instant residential pricing and booking/payment. Large villas remain standard residential, not automatically custom by size.

**How to apply:** Keep instant area-based prices limited to eligible single-property residential services. Do not offer combined instant pricing even for two units. Preserve authoritative area pricing and VAT for eligible work.
