---
name: Visibility methodology
description: Defines the evidence boundary between website AI readiness and observed AI-platform visibility.
---

GEO readiness may score crawlability, semantic clarity, structured data, citability, and entity signals. LLM visibility must remain empty until supported AI platforms are directly queried and their responses are stored.

**Why:** Website optimization signals do not prove that ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, or another answer engine mentions, recommends, or cites UrbanGrid.

**How to apply:** Never infer LLM mention, recommendation, citation, position, sentiment, or share-of-voice metrics from SEO, schema, backlinks, search results, or GEO checks. Label unavailable providers “Not Connected.”
